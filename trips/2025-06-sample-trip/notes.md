This is a **sample trip** showing how the journal works — delete this folder
(`trips/2025-06-sample-trip/`) and run `python publish.py` once you've added
a real trip.

Write anything here: the story of the trip, favorite meals, who you were
with, what you'd do differently next time. Markdown formatting works:

- Lists for the highlights
- *Italics* and **bold** for emphasis
- [Links](https://example.com) to places, maps, or restaurants

> Blockquotes are nice for a memory or a quote from a travel companion.
